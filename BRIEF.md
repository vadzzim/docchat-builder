# Embeddable Chatbot Builder
MVP of a real product

An app that lets users upload company docs and knowledge and turn it into a chatbot. The chatbot is available inside the app as a ChatGPT-like chat interface and as an embeddable widget, so customers can embed it into their websites and their users can ask questions.

Pricing and billing flow are included (Stripe test account and credentials or mock billing). Pricing and gated features are defined in whatever way makes sense.

The app is treated as a real product MVP ready to launch and serve real users, even though there are no live payments.

Additional features, such as a specific niche or useful capabilities, can be added while keeping the app meaningful and usable.

## Suggested Stack
- Supabase for storing data, user auth and vector search
- Any AI API for serving the chatbot (ChatGPT/Gemini or other)

## Deliverables
- Landing page showcasing the product features and pricing
- Web app implementing the features above
- Presentation: video walkthrough with screenshare and voiceover (preferred) or written tutorial with screenshots

## Criteria on the project check
- No useless features, focused scope
- Descriptive landing page, showcasing the product just enough so users want to test it out
- Functioning app and working features
- Attention to detail (copywriting, design, feel, functionality)
